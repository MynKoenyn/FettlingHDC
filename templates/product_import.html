Upload a Product Sheet

Start from the template below, fill it in, and upload it here. CSV or Excel both work.

How rows are matched
  • A row is matched to an existing product by Product Code, falling back to Name — matched products are updated, the rest are added. Re-sending a corrected file is safe.
  • A blank cell never blanks a field; it leaves what is already there. So a sheet of just Product Code and Weight is a safe way to top up one field across the catalogue.
  • Customers and departments must already exist — they are matched by name, never created here. Anything unresolved is listed after the import and the rest of the row still loads.
  • Current Price and Price per kg are not importable. Both are worked out for display — the price comes from the price list, and price per kg is that ÷ the weight.

Template Columns

The headings the importer looks for. Column order does not matter, and anything it doesn't recognise is ignored.

ColumnNotes
NamerequiredProduct name. Required for a new product; on an update it renames the matched one.
Product CodeYour casting/part code. Used first to match the row to an existing product.
Supplier CodeThe customer's own code for the item.
Supplier DescriptionFree text.
GradeOne of {{ grades | join(', ') }}. Anything else is reported and left unset.
PriceCatalogue price. Used for price per kg only when the product has no price-list entry.
Weight (kg)Cast weight. The divisor behind price per kg.
Primary CustomerCustomer name, exactly as it appears on the Customers page.
Linked CustomersExtra customers, separated by ;. Replaces the existing set when the cell has a value.
DepartmentsSeparated by ;. Where a department name exists in more than one division, qualify it — HDC — Fettling.
BarcodeFree text.
Stock ItemYes / No — is this product held as stock, or made to order? New products default to Yes.
ActiveYes / No — No keeps the product and its history but takes it off the product pickers. New products default to Yes.
