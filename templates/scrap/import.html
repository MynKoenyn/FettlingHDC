Expected Columns

Headings are matched loosely — spacing, capitalisation and the explanatory text under a heading are all ignored. % Reject and Monthly Summary are calculated here, so they are read past. A row is treated as a duplicate when its customer, machined part number, casting number, batch and reject date all match a row already loaded. Duplicates are never imported silently — they are listed on the import's own screen with the row they clash with, and you allow or reject each one.
