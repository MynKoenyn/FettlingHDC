Overview

The Furnace Management System records every heat produced on the melt floor: which furnace and lining it ran on, who ran it, what was charged into it, when each stage of the melt happened, and what the lab measured. It also carries the tin & copper trim calculation, live temperature data from MeltControl, and reporting over the whole lot.

A normal shift looks like this:

  • Entry — open a heat on a furnace, capture materials, press the four process buttons as they happen, record each tap, then save.
  • Lab Linker — attach the base and final spectro samples to that heat.
  • Sn & Cu — work out the tin and copper additions and keep the daily stock straight.
  • Reports — supervisors pull cycle times, material usage and lab results afterwards.
Logging In & Roles

Every page except the login screen requires a signed-in user. Tick Remember Me on the login form to stay signed in — sessions last 30 days and refresh on each use, so a shop-floor terminal will not log itself out mid-shift.

Two roles exist, set per user by an administrator:

Role Can use
Operator Dashboard, Entry, Sn & Cu, Temp Data, Lab Linker, entry detail views
Admin Everything above, plus Furnaces, Personnel, Metal Grades, History, Reports and Users

Your name and role show in the top-right of the navigation bar. Log out from that same dropdown. A deactivated account cannot log in — an administrator has to switch it back on.

Dashboard

The landing page after login. It answers "what is happening right now" in one screen:

  • Summary tiles — active furnaces, active personnel, metal grades on file, total entries, and entries recorded today.
  • Per-furnace cards — for each active furnace, the materials accumulated today across every heat on that furnace, plus how many heats have run.
  • Combined totals — the same material breakdown summed across all furnaces for the day.
  • Jump to the open heat — if a furnace has an entry still In Progress, the card links straight into it, so an operator returning to a terminal lands on the right heat.
Dashboard totals cover today only and reset at midnight. For any other period use Reports or History.
Entries List

Entry in the navigation bar lists every heat, newest first, and is where new heats are started.

Filters. Narrow the list by furnace, metal grade, and a from/to date range.

Row actions.

  • View — the read-only detail page with timings, taps and lab results.
  • Edit — reopen the entry form to keep capturing or to correct it.
  • Lab Linker — attach spectro samples to the heat.

Starting a new heat. Choose the furnace in the new-entry dialog. The system creates the entry with today's date, the furnace's current lining number, and the standard starting charge (1700 kg cast iron, 300 kg steel scrap, 0 kg pig iron), then drops you straight into the entry form.

One open heat per furnace. If that furnace already has an entry that is not Completed, no second entry is created — you are redirected into the existing one. Finish or correct that heat first.
Recording a Heat (the Entry Form)

The entry form is the core of the system. The left side captures what went into the furnace; the right side captures when things happened.

1. Basic Information

Heat number (digits only, e.g. 0408), furnace, metal grade, melt technician, furnace operator and lining number. The four dropdowns are required before the entry can be saved as complete — the technician and operator lists only show active personnel in the matching role, and the furnace list only shows active furnaces.

2. Base Materials (kg)

Cast iron, steel scrap, pig iron, recarb, ferrosilicon, ferromanganese and iron sulfide — the charge made up at the start of the melt.

3. Additional Materials (kg)

Additional recarb, FeSi, FeMn and iron sulfide, plus tin and copper — the corrections made after the base sample comes back. Use the Sn & Cu calculator to work out the tin and copper figures.

4. Process Details

Melt temperature (accepted between 1300°C and 1560°C) and free-text remarks for anything the fields do not cover — delays, breakdowns, quality notes.

5. Process Timestamps — press as it happens

These four buttons stamp the current time the moment you press them. Press them in real time; do not fill them in afterwards from memory, because every cycle-time report is built from them.

Button Press it when…
Step 1 — Start Charging You begin filling the furnace with metal.
Step 2 — End Melt The furnace is full and ready for the base sample to be taken.
Step 3 — Additions Added The final additions have been made after the final sample.
Step 4 — Tap Times Each pot is tapped. Opens a dialog to record that tap — press it once per pot.

The tap dialog records the tap time along with its temperature, which inoculant was used, and where the metal is going (HDA, Unit 1, Unit 2, Unit 3 or Floor Moulding), so each pot carries its own reading rather than one figure for the whole heat.

6. Autosave

The form saves itself in the background: whenever you type or change a field, whenever you press a timestamp button, and every 20 seconds regardless. The Autosave line under the timestamp buttons shows when the last save went through. A dropped connection or a closed browser does not lose the heat — reopen it from the Entries list or the dashboard and carry on.

7. Save Entry

Save Entry completes the heat. Three things happen: the status changes to Completed, the furnace's lining counter is incremented by one, and you are returned to the Entries list. Only completed entries are counted in Reports.

Because saving bumps the lining number, do not press Save Entry repeatedly on the same heat to "re-save" it — every save advances that furnace's lining count. Autosave keeps your work safe while the heat is still running.
Entry Detail

The read-only view of a single heat. It brings together:

  • All captured data — personnel, materials, process details and remarks.
  • Tap list — every tap with its temperature, inoculant flag, and the gap since the previous tap.
  • Calculated durations — melt time, corrections time, furnace emptying time and full melt time (defined under Field Reference).
  • Lab results — the base and final spectro samples linked to this heat.
  • Timeline chart — this heat drawn as a bar with markers for additions, end melt and each tap.
  • Concurrent activity — heats running on other furnaces whose window overlaps this one, so you can see what else the floor was doing at the time.

The page prints cleanly — navigation and buttons are dropped from the printout.

Lab Linker

Spectrometer results arrive in the system on their own, without knowing which heat they belong to. The Lab Linker is where you make that connection: open it from the button on any row of the Entries list.

You are shown two lists of unlinked results, newest first:

  • BASE — the sample taken when the furnace was full, before corrections.
  • FINAL — the sample taken after the corrections were made.

Pick one of each and save. Both are attached to the heat and disappear from the unlinked lists, and the results appear on that heat's detail page and in the Reports lab tables. Match on measurement date and time against the heat's own timestamps if the heat number on the sample is ambiguous.

Sn & Cu Calculator

Works out how much tin and copper to add to hit the grade target, and tracks the daily stock of both. Records are listed newest first and can be filtered by furnace, grade and date range.

Creating a record

Capture the date, heat number, operator, furnace, grade and the ladle weight (250, 500 or 2000 kg), then enter the base tin % and base copper % from the base spectro sample. The system calculates the kilograms to be added from the grade target; you then enter what was actually added, which is what the stock figures work from.

Grade targets
Grade Tin target Copper target
GG250.075 %0.20 %
GG300.075 %0.90 %
SG50no tin added0.40 %
SG60no tin added0.45 %

The addition is (target − base) × weight, allowing for the purity of the material. A grade outside this list has no target on file, so check the figure by hand before adding.

Stock tracking

Starting stock on a new record is carried forward automatically from the previous record: previous starting + previous issued − previous added. Enter Issued only on the record covering the day's draw from the store — tin and copper issued may be recorded once per day only. If issued figures already exist for that date, the system refuses the save and tells you which record already holds them.

Temp Data

A live read-only window onto the MeltControl measurement database — a separate system. Nothing here is edited or stored by the Furnace Management System.

  • Filters — date range (defaults to today) and measuring station.
  • Column picker — tick only the columns you care about. Untick everything and you get them all back.
  • Chart — temperature against time, split by station. It always plots timestamp, station and temperature no matter which columns are ticked.
  • Table — capped at 500 rows on screen so the page stays fast.
  • Export CSV — exports the full filtered result with no row cap, using your chosen columns. The file opens correctly in Excel including degree symbols in the headers.
If this page shows a connection error, MeltControl is unreachable rather than the Furnace system being down — every other page keeps working.
History Admin only

A read-only archive of every entry with the same furnace / grade / date filters as the Entries list, but no edit or delete actions — use it for looking things up without any risk of changing a record. Each row links through to its detail page.

Reports Admin only

Choose a date range, optionally a furnace, and optionally a time-of-day window, then generate. Only completed entries are included — heats still in progress are left out.

Shift filtering

The time window filters on Start Charging clock time, so it isolates a shift across the whole date range. It handles a window that crosses midnight — 22:00 to 06:00 gives you the night shift. Setting both times the same (or leaving them blank) means a full 24 hours, i.e. no time filtering.

What you get
  • Headline figures — total entries, total materials, average materials per heat, and the date range covered.
  • Material usage breakdown — consumption per material across the period.
  • Furnace usage statistics — how the work was split between furnaces.
  • Cycle time statistics — melt, corrections, emptying and full melt times.
  • Detailed entries table — every heat in the period, each linking to its detail page.
  • Lab results — spectro readings for the period across all 30 measured elements, exportable to CSV.

Reports also links across to the Furnace Activity Report described next.

Furnace Activity Report

A swim-lane timeline — one lane per furnace, each heat drawn as a coloured bar from start charging to its last tap, with markers for additions, end melt and each tap along the way. It is the quickest way to see furnace utilisation, overlaps and gaps across a shift or a week.

  • Reached from the button at the top of the Reports page, or directly at /reports/furnace-activity.
  • Pick any date range; it defaults to today.
  • Overnight melts are handled — a heat that starts before the range or runs past the end of it still appears, as long as its active window overlaps the range.
  • Each furnace keeps its own colour, and day bands separate the dates on a multi-day range.
  • A heat only appears once it has a Start Charging timestamp.

The page carries the generation time, so a printed copy is self-documenting.

Setup Data Admin only

These three lists feed the dropdowns on every other page. Keep them tidy and the rest of the system stays tidy.

Furnaces
  • Name (must be unique), capacity and unit (tons or kg).
  • Current lining number — stamped onto each new heat and advanced automatically every time an entry is saved. Set it by hand only when a lining is physically replaced.
  • Status — Active, Maintenance or Inactive. Only Active furnaces can have new heats started on them or appear on the dashboard.
Personnel
  • Name, unique clock number, and role — Melt Technician or Furnace Operator. The role decides which dropdown a person appears in on the entry form.
  • Deactivate rather than delete when someone leaves — they drop out of the dropdowns while their history stays intact.
Metal Grades
  • Grade name (must be unique), description and notes.
  • The name matters: the Sn & Cu calculator matches on it exactly (GG25, GG30, SG50, SG60) to pick the target. A renamed or misspelt grade silently loses its target.
Users Admin only

Create login accounts, set first and last name, choose the role (Operator or Admin), and set or reset a password — minimum six characters, and required when creating a new account. Usernames must be unique.

Accounts are deactivated, not deleted. A deactivated user is refused at login but every entry they captured keeps their name against it. The list shows each user's role and last login.

Melt Time Start Charging → End Melt

Furnace Emptying Time First tap → last tap
Full Melt Time Start Charging → last tap

All are shown as Hh Mm Ss. A duration that cannot be worked out — because a timestamp was never pressed — shows as 0h 0m 0s. This is the single biggest reason to press the buttons in real time.

Entry status
  • In Progress — the heat is open. It blocks a new entry on that furnace, and it is excluded from Reports.
  • Completed — Save Entry has been pressed. Counted in Reports; the lining number has been advanced.

Lab elements

Spectro results carry 30 elements: C, Si, Mn, P, S, Cr, Mo, Ni, Al, Co, Cu, Nb, Ti, V, W, Pb, Sn, Mg, As, Zr, Bi, Ce, Sb, Se, Te, B, Zn, La, N and Fe, each as a percentage, along with the sample type (BASE or FINAL), pot number and measurement date and time.

Troubleshooting
"There is already an entry in progress for this furnace."
That furnace has an open heat. You were redirected into it — finish it with Save Entry, or correct it, before starting another.
The entry will not save.
Furnace, metal grade, melt technician and furnace operator are all required. The heat number must be digits only, and the melt temperature must fall between 1300°C and 1560°C. The page shows which field is at fault at the top.
A person or furnace is missing from a dropdown.
They are deactivated, or the person is filed under the other role. An administrator can fix this under Personnel or Furnaces.
A duration shows as 0h 0m 0s.
One of the timestamps it depends on was never captured. Reopen the entry and check which of the four steps is blank.
Lab results are not showing against a heat.
They have not been linked yet. Open the Lab Linker from that heat's row in the Entries list and attach the base and final samples.
"Tin/Copper Issued already recorded for this date."
Issued quantities are recorded once per day. The message names the record that already holds them — edit that record instead of adding a second one.
Temp Data shows a connection error.
The MeltControl database is unreachable. This is external to the Furnace system; report it to IT. Everything else keeps working.
The lining number looks too high.
It advances by one on every entry save for that furnace. An administrator can set the correct value on the furnace record.
